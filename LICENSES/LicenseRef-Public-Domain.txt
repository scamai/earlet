This identifier marks a work in this repository that is in the public domain
and carries no license. REUSE.toml names the files it covers.

It covers the web2 word list: the headwords of Webster's Second International
Dictionary (1934), as distributed with BSD and macOS in /usr/share/dict/web2,
gzip-compressed. Its provenance is the statement in the BSD share/dict
README, by James A. Woods:

    Welcome to web2 (Webster's Second International) all 234,936 words worth.
    The 1934 copyright has lapsed, according to the supplier.
